# 1. Goal and boundaries

## Goal

Execute the frozen campaign and end with a dataset that someone else can check:

- every registered sample collected in full (§4 table), failed trials included;
- one immutable evidence bundle per run, with its manifest and hashes;
- every AWS window closed with teardown and a clean inventory;
- raw analysis reproduced from the files by a script, with a decision of
  `PASS`, `FAIL` or `INCONCLUSIVE` for each of H1–H6;
- delayed billing appended as versioned files, then a dataset freeze and a tag.

This is the ver3 §12 exit criterion for P9: "Đủ sample theo contract; giữ failed
trials, run manifests và raw evidence", and for the cost append: "Chỉ freeze khi
đủ sample và cost evidence; thiếu dữ liệu vẫn PENDING/INCONCLUSIVE".

A hypothesis that is not met does not make P9 fail (ver3 §4.3). An incomplete,
edited or unreproducible dataset does.

## MUST deliverables

- `SPEC-P9` approved by the owner with decisions D1–D13 (§9.1) resolved.
- Campaign start record: freeze record and tag verified, preconditions rechecked
  on the day, window plan and priced estimate approved.
- Evidence bundles for every official run under
  `experiments/runs/<campaign_id>/<run_id>/` in the layout of EXPERIMENT_PLAN
  §8.1; redacted derivatives in Git, raw files kept as D5 says.
- Interaction sheets for the ten manual baseline runs.
- Per-window record: approval, run list, deviations, teardown, inventory,
  cost reading.
- `trial_ledger.csv`, `summary_by_experiment.csv`, `hypothesis_decisions.json`,
  charts, the matched cost-pair table and the recovery table (EXPERIMENT_PLAN
  §9.3), generated by the frozen analysis script.
- Exclusion record: every excluded run with its code, evidence and the approval
  given before the primary analysis.
- Billing append files with version and timestamp; updated `COST_LEDGER.md`.
- `dataset_freeze.json`, the secret-scan result and the Git tag of D9.
- Evidence index under `docs/evidence/p9/`.

## Negative boundaries

1. Do not start an official trial without the approved freeze record, its tag
   and a campaign start record dated that day.
2. Do not change code, manifests, images, versions, profiles, timeouts, success
   rules or exclusion codes during the campaign. A material change creates a
   new campaign version whose trials are never mixed with the old one
   (EXPERIMENT_PLAN §12.2).
3. Do not delete, overwrite or edit a trial, an event or a timestamp. Redaction
   makes a new file and keeps a hash of the raw one.
4. Do not exclude a trial for a reason that is not a registered code, and never
   because its result is bad. Timeouts, capacity errors and controller errors
   after `t0` are valid failures and stay in the denominator
   (EXPERIMENT_PLAN §11.1).
5. Do not stop a block early because the result is already clear, and do not add
   trials beyond the registered count except replacements for excluded ones.
6. Do not form or re-form cost pairs after seeing a cost. The mapping is the one
   in the freeze record.
7. Do not label an On-Demand run as Spot, a CPU run as GPU, or a warm-up or pilot
   run as official.
8. Do not write zero for billing that has not arrived. Do not freeze the dataset
   while a required cost is still pending unless the affected result is marked
   `PENDING` or `INCONCLUSIVE`.
9. Do not write a number into the report, a slide or a script by hand. Results
   come from the analysis output only, and only after the dataset freeze are they
   written as results (ver3 §15.1).
10. Do not keep AWS resources between windows, overnight, or "to save setup
    time". Each window has its own approval, cost check, stop time and teardown
    (ver3 §12).
11. Do not let an agent perform a manual baseline or give an approval. Those are
    the owner's.
12. Do not put account IDs, ARNs, endpoints, bucket names, addresses or secrets
    into a committed bundle.
13. Do not reduce a sample, a timeout, cleanup or the learning gate to keep a
    date (ver3 §12.1). If the plan does not fit, the date moves.

# 3. Learning objectives

By the end of P9 the owner should be able to:

1. Explain for any run in the ledger why it is included, failed or excluded.
2. Compute p50 and nearest-rank p95 by hand for one experiment and say what 20
   or 5 samples can and cannot support.
3. Explain why a timeout after `t0` counts against the success rate.
4. Explain why the rules were frozen before the data and what would have biased
   the result otherwise.
5. Read each hypothesis decision and state the result with its limits, including
   the ones that are not met.
6. Explain why the cost result waited for billing and what `PENDING` means.

# 11. Open risks

- **Dependence on P8.** Without the harness, the pilot and the freeze this SPEC
  cannot start, and its plan numbers are placeholders.
- **Spot capacity during the batch block.** Capacity errors are valid failures
  and can cost H3 for batch; fallbacks can leave fewer than five Spot pairs for
  H5. Both are results to report. Extra pairs to reach five Spot pairs are
  allowed only if the frozen contract says so in advance.
- **A new cluster every window.** Twenty runs of one experiment may span two
  clusters. The window is recorded on every run and results are also shown per
  window; a cold image cache at the start of a window is handled as the freeze
  record says.
- **Operator effect.** Ten manual runs by one person get faster with practice;
  report the sequence, not only the mean.
- **Long days.** Windows 2–4 are close to 8 hours. Fatigue causes protocol
  violations, which cost a run and a replacement. A shorter day with one more
  window is the safer plan if the rebaseline allows it.
- **Session expiry inside a run** (D13).
- **Billing lag and granularity.** The H5 decision may stay `PENDING` past the
  planned freeze. The append rule exists for this.
- **Evidence loss.** One machine holds the raw files during the campaign; D5
  asks for a second copy the same day.
- **Price or support change.** A price change after the freeze is recorded per
  run, not corrected. EKS 1.35 leaving standard support would multiply the
  control-plane price by six (A7).
- **Budget category overrun** (§10.3).
- **Schedule.** P9 cannot start on its ver3 date; the rebaseline is the
  owner's, and the sample sizes do not move with it.
