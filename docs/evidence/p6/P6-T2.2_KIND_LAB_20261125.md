## Findings

1. **kro pruned the Jobs KEDA created (fixed).** First run: step 2 showed `jobs=0`
   with a running Pod, then a new Job every 10 s, blocked only by quota D9. KEDA
   copies every ScaledJob label onto its Jobs (`scale_jobs.go`, v2.21.1), including
   kro's `applyset.kubernetes.io/part-of` and `kro.run/*`. The instance's applyset
   lists `Job.batch` (the submit Job) for namespace `tenant-a`, so kro pruned each
   KEDA Job as a stale member and left its Pod without an owner. Fix: annotation
   `scaledjob.keda.sh/job-excluded-labels` on the ScaledJob; `check.sh` rule 20
   requires it; guard case "KEDA Jobs join kro's applyset". After the fix, steps
   2-6 above. **Quota D9 is what stopped the loop**: without it each poll would
   have added a Pod (on EKS, possibly a node).
2. **The 4th Job of a `fail-always` request is `Complete`.** SQS moves a message to
   the DLQ on the 4th receive, so the 4th Job receives nothing, logs `NO_MESSAGE`
   and exits 0. A `Complete` Job is not a training outcome (boundary 8); the suite
   must read markers, never Job status. On EKS this is one extra short Pod.
3. **The lab bucket ConfigMap needs the `platformConfig` edge.** Without a
   reference to `batch-config` it was created before the gate opened. The lab node
   now carries `region: ${platformConfig.data.region}`, the same edge the real
   Crossplane bucket has through `forProvider.region` and tags.
4. **Job vs Pod labels.** KEDA labels Jobs `scaledjob.keda.sh/name=<scaledjob>`;
   `app.kubernetes.io/name=batch-train` is on the Pod template only. Job counts
   and the guarded wait (§9.3 L1) select by the KEDA label.
5. **PodSecurity warnings** on the quota probe Jobs (`suspend: true`, no Pod) are
   expected and do not mean a violation.
